Speed, Weather & Stopping Distance

Posted speed limits assume dry pavement, good visibility, and light traffic. The safe speed in any condition is whatever lets you stop within the distance you can clearly see ahead.

Curves & Corners

Slow down before you enter a curve, not while you are in it. Braking mid-curve can break traction. Reduce speed on the approach, then accelerate gently through the turn to keep the vehicle settled and stable.
